# Memory Screen Saver Plus X - v2.9.51 Release Notes

**Release date:** 2026-11-04

## Changes

- **Audio support added.** Play a background soundtrack from your own music folders or files while photos and videos show, in both the screensaver and live presentation. Turn it on with *Play audio* on the new Audio tab; choose sources, volume and loudness there. The soundtrack pauses when a video with sound is playing or another app is playing audio. Music stays on your device. (Not available on Apple Silicon Macs yet.)
- Smoother startup: the video/audio engine now warms up in the background so Settings and the screensaver no longer wait on it.
- Better diagnostics: errors now log the recent activity leading up to them, making problems easier to report and fix.
- Interface translations refreshed across all 14 languages.

## Requirements

| Platform | Minimum OS |
|----------|-----------|
| Windows  | Windows 10 (1809+), x64 or arm64 |
| macOS    | macOS 12 Monterey, x64 (Intel) or arm64 (Apple Silicon) |
